Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't it find any info for my plate?

CarInfo AR queries the official government registry of the country you're scanning in. Coverage today: Israel (data.gov.il), Netherlands (RDW), United Kingdom (DVLA). If your plate isn't in those registries — or the registry is temporarily down — the lookup will come back empty.

Make sure the country flag in the toolbar matches the plate's country. You can switch it manually from Settings → Country.

UK plates aren't working — what do I do?

UK lookups go through the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service, which requires a free API key. Go to the DVLA VES page, request a key, then paste it into the app under Settings → DVLA API key.

I tapped "Buy" to remove ads but nothing happens.

Make sure you're signed into the App Store (or Google Play on Android) with a payment method on file, and that you have an internet connection. The store sheet usually appears within a second of tapping Buy.

If it still doesn't appear, force-quit and relaunch the app — the store catalog is fetched on launch and may take a moment after first install. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and OS version.

I bought "Remove ads" but the ads are still showing.

Open Settings → Premium and tap the row again. The receipt is restored automatically on every launch via Apple/Google's standard restore flow. If you still see ads after a fresh launch, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll fix it.

How do I switch country?

Tap the country flag in the camera toolbar to switch between Israel, Netherlands, and United Kingdom — or open Settings → Country to set a manual default. The app also auto-detects your country from your device locale on first launch.

Will more countries be added?

Yes — we're working on expanding coverage. Country support depends on the local government making vehicle registry data available via a public API. If you'd like a specific country added, email us and we'll prioritize based on demand.

What does the app know about me?

Camera frames are processed entirely on-device and never leave your phone. The plate text is sent to the relevant government registry over HTTPS and is not stored by us. So that we can send you a message inside the app, the app has a random anonymous ID and tells us when it is open, with your region, language and app version — no name, phone, e-mail or location, no tracking across apps, and no analytics on your scans. Full details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my scan history?

Open the History screen, swipe a row from right to left to delete a single entry, or tap the trash icon in the top-right to clear all history. Scan history is stored only on your device — there's nothing to delete on our side.
