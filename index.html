Why doesn't it find any info for my plate?

CarInfo AR queries the official government registry of the country you're scanning in. Coverage today: Israel (data.gov.il), Netherlands (RDW), United Kingdom (DVLA). If your plate isn't in those registries — or the registry is temporarily down — the lookup will come back empty.

Make sure the country flag in the toolbar matches the plate's country. You can switch it manually from Settings → Country.

UK plates aren't working — what do I do?

UK lookups go through the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service, which requires a free API key. Go to the DVLA VES page, request a key, then paste it into the app under Settings → DVLA API key.

I tapped "Buy" to remove ads but nothing happens.

Make sure you're signed into the App Store / Google Play with a payment method on file, and that you have an internet connection. The store sheet usually appears within a second of tapping Buy.

If it still doesn't appear, force-quit and relaunch the app — the store catalog is fetched on launch and may take a moment after first install. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and OS version.

I bought "Remove ads" but the ads are still showing.

Open Settings → Premium and tap the row again. The receipt is restored automatically on every launch via Apple/Google's standard restore flow. If you still see ads after a fresh launch, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll fix it.

What does the app know about me?

Camera frames are processed entirely on-device and never leave your phone. The plate text is sent to the relevant government registry over HTTPS and is not stored by us. So that we can send you a message inside the app, the app has a random anonymous ID and tells us when it is open, with your region, language and app version — no name, phone, e-mail or location, no tracking across apps, and no analytics on your scans. 2. How We Use Information

  • Camera images are analyzed in real time on your device to detect and read license plate text. No image data leaves your device.
  • Detected plate numbers are sent to the relevant government vehicle API (based on your selected country) to retrieve publicly available vehicle information.
  • Scan history is maintained locally so you can revisit previous lookups without re-scanning.
  • Preferences are stored locally to remember your chosen country and language settings between sessions.
  • App status (anonymous ID, platform, region, language, version, when the app was open) is used to reach you with a message inside the app and to keep the service working. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in supporting the people who use the app and in running the service. It is never used for advertising and never linked to your name or phone.

3. Third-Party Services

CarInfo AR integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing data it may collect:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements. May collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. You may opt out of personalized ads through your device's settings.
  • App Store / Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time in-app purchase. We do not collect or store your payment information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics — monitors stability and anonymous usage patterns. No personally identifiable information collected. Firebase Privacy.
  • On-device text recognition — license plate OCR runs entirely on your device.
  • Government Vehicle APIs — license plate numbers are sent to:
    • Israel — data.gov.il
    • Netherlands — RDW Open Data
    • United Kingdom — DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service
  • Our copies of public vehicle registers (Google Cloud Storage) — Denmark and Ukraine have no per-plate government service, so the app looks plates up in our own copy of their public registers: Denmark — Motorstyrelsen (DMR) statistics extract; Ukraine — МВС України / data.gov.ua, licensed CC BY 4.0. The same kind of copy serves Israel and the Netherlands when the government service does not answer. These copies contain vehicle data only (no owner or person data), and the app downloads a file chosen by a number computed from the plate — the plate itself is not sent. Each result names its source and the date the copy describes.

3a. Listings — publishing your car for sale (optional)

If you choose to publish your car from My car, and only then, the following is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and shown to other CarInfo AR users:

  • What is shown to everyone using the app: your car's details from the public registry (make, model, year, fuel), and what you type: price, kilometres, city, a note, and any photos you add.
  • Photos (optional, up to 5): shown to everyone with your listing. Before a photo leaves your phone, the app redraws it from its pixels, which removes the location, time and camera details stored in the original file, and blurs any licence plate it can read. The blurring is automatic and can miss a plate — check your photos before you publish. Photos are stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Storage, in the EU) and deleted together with your listing, and in any case within 32 days.
  • Where a photo was taken (fraud checks only): the app may keep the name of the city a photo was taken in, worked out on your phone from the original photo by your phone’s own location service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone); the exact position is never sent to us or stored. It is not shown to anyone and is deleted with the listing.
  • What is shown only to someone who opens your listing and taps “Show contact”: your car's licence plate and the phone number you entered. The phone number is not verified — only enter a number that is yours; you are responsible for it.
  • Identity: the app signs in anonymously (a random identifier, no name, e-mail or account) so that only you can delete your listing.
  • Blocking abuse: each listing also stores a one-way code made from an identifier of your device (the identifier itself never leaves your phone). It is not shown to anyone and is deleted with the listing. If a device keeps posting abusive or false listings, we can block that code so the app can no longer publish from that device; a blocked code is kept until we lift the block.
  • Retention: a listing, its photos and its contact details are deleted automatically 30 days after publishing (the deletion can run up to one day late). You can delete your listing at any time from My car.
  • Moderation: listings are checked automatically once a day, including by an AI model we run ourselves; listings that are reported or break the rules are hidden, and their contact details and photos are deleted. Reports are stored without being shown to anyone else.
  • A listing you can no longer reach (for example after reinstalling the app): write to carinfo.ar.app@gmail.com and we will remove it.
  • If you never publish, nothing in this section applies to you and nothing is sent.

3b. Listing rules (terms of use for publishing)

By tapping “I agree” before your first listing, you accept these rules. They apply to everything you publish and to how you use other people’s listings.

  • Only your own car, for sale, by you. Publish only a vehicle you own or are entitled to sell. No ads for other goods or services, jobs, loans, dealers’ stock lists or anything that is not a private car for sale.
  • Only your own phone number. Enter a number that is yours. Publishing someone else’s number is not allowed; people can report it and it will be hidden.
  • Contact details go in the phone field only. No phone numbers, e-mail addresses, links, usernames or messaging handles in the price, city or note.
  • Zero tolerance for objectionable content. No insults, hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, or text about a person rather than the car. Listings with such content are hidden and their contact details deleted.
  • Honest details. The car’s details come from the public registry; the price, kilometres and note you add must be true to the best of your knowledge.
  • Reporting and blocking. Any user can report a listing from the listing itself, and can hide a seller’s listings on their own device. Reported listings are usually reviewed within 24 hours; listings that break these rules are hidden and their contact details deleted.
  • Using a listing’s contact details. Use a seller’s phone number only to ask about that car. Collecting, reselling or messaging numbers in bulk is not allowed.
  • Removal. We may hide or remove any listing that breaks these rules, without notice.
  • Before you uninstall. Delete your listing in My car first. The app signs in anonymously, so after uninstalling it can no longer reach your listing, which then stays up until it expires (at most 30 days) — or e-mail us and we will remove it.
  • We are not a party to the sale. CarInfo AR shows listings; it does not check the cars, the sellers or the buyers, and is not responsible for any deal made through a listing. Check a car and its documents before you pay.
  • Abuse contact: carinfo.ar.app@gmail.com — for a listing that uses your number, a listing you can no longer reach, or anything else that breaks these rules.

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third party, except as described above (government API queries for vehicle lookups and AdMob for advertising).
