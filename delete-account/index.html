Delete your account

CarInfo AR needs no account to scan plates or to publish one private car. Only dealers who register create an account (with an e-mail address). This page explains how to delete it and what is deleted.

In the app (fastest)

Open Settings › Delete dealer account and confirm. The account and everything listed below are deleted at once.

Without the app

Write to support@carinfoar.com from the e-mail address you registered with, with the subject Delete my dealer account. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your sign-in (e-mail address), your dealer record (business name, package status), the cars in My cars, every listing you published — as a dealer, and your own private car listing if you have one — with its photos and contact details, the private copy of your phone number (privacy policy, section 3a), and your «for sale» tags. Messages follow their own rule: each one is deleted 90 days after it was sent (see the privacy policy, section 3d).

What is kept

Records of store payments (order number, package, dates) that we must keep for accounting. They hold no card or payment details — those stay with Google Play or the App Store.

Subscriptions: deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or on your iPhone (Settings › your name › Subscriptions).
